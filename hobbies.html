🎨 My Hobbies

Activities and interests that bring joy to my life

Photography

Capturing moments and telling stories through images

Photography has been my passion for over 5 years. I love capturing landscapes, street scenes, and candid moments during my travels. There's something magical about freezing a moment in time and being able to revisit it years later. My favorite subjects are sunrise/sunset landscapes and authentic street photography that captures the essence of local culture.

📷 5+ years experience
🏆 Local photo contest winner

Fitness & Running

Maintaining health and pushing physical limits

Staying active is essential for both physical and mental health. I run 3-4 times a week, usually early in the morning when the air is fresh and the city is quiet. Running helps me clear my mind, stay energized, and maintain focus throughout the day. I've completed several 5K and 10K races, and I'm currently training for my first half-marathon.
